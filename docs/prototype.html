Ⅴ · Quaestiones — frequent

Questions, answered.

The fine print reads like the headline, so here it is in FAQ form.

Is it actually free? What's the catch?

Truly free — MIT licensed, no premium tier, no trial, no watermark. It's young open software; bug reports are the exchange, that's all.

Does it send my videos anywhere?

No. The editor runs 100% locally: no accounts, no telemetry, no network calls. Drop an NDA project inside; it never leaves your disk.

Why does Windows warn me when installing?

Unsigned open-source executables read as "unknown" to SmartScreen. Click More info → Run anyway. The source is public; you can rebuild the installer yourself to byte-compare.

How do updates work?

The app soft-checks Releases at startup and tells you when a newer build exists — one click to download; installers upgrade in place.

Can I help without code?

Yes: test the installer on your hardware, file one good issue, or pick a good first issue. The marathon is held in public on GitHub.
